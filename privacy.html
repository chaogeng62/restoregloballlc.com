Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Restore Global LLC ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Instant Games on the Meta platforms, including Facebook and Messenger. This Privacy Policy explains what information our games collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Gameplay information: such as game progress, scores, and settings, used to operate and improve our games.
  • Device and technical information: such as device type, operating system, and language, used for compatibility and troubleshooting.
  • Information you provide: such as emails you send to our support address.

Our games run on Meta's Instant Games platform, which may separately collect information under Meta's own privacy policies.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve our games;
  • To provide player support and respond to inquiries;
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our games, or when required by law.

Children

Our games are general-audience casual games. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you.
